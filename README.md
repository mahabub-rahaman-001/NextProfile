One profile in → Resume + CV + Portfolio + Public Career Profile + PDF + QR out.

The idea in one paragraph

A person enters their education, experience, skills, projects, research and achievements once. NextProfile stores that as a structured career record, helps improve the writing with AI, and renders it into whatever format the moment requires — a one-page resume for a job application, an academic CV for a PhD, a portfolio website for clients, a public link for a business card. Change a job title once and every output updates.

The three ideas everything rests on
Universal Profile — one record holds a person's entire career: education, experience, skills, projects, achievements, research, services.
Dynamic Experience — fields, templates, themes, recommendations and AI tone all adapt to the user's profile type and career goal. One application, not five.
Reusable Output Engine — the same record renders as a resume, a CV, a portfolio, a public profile, a job-specific resume, a PDF and a QR code. New templates ship without touching the database.
MVP in one line

A non-technical person signs up, answers three questions, fills in their background, gets AI help writing it, picks a template, previews it live, publishes a public link, and downloads a PDF — in under 15 minutes, on a phone.
